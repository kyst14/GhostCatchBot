# Privacy Policy for Ghost Catch Bot (@ghostcatchbot)

**Last updated:** May 28, 2026

## Overview

**Ghost Catch Bot** is a Telegram bot that helps users save deleted, edited, and other messages in chats.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## What Data We Collect

We collect the following data only when you use the bot through **Business Connection**:

- **Messages** (text, captions)
- **Media files** (photos, videos) — where permitted by Telegram’s platform capabilities ("protected content")
- Message metadata (message ID, chat ID, sender ID, timestamps)
- Business Connection ID and owner information

We **do not** collect:
- Your personal phone number
- Passwords
- Payment information
- Location data (unless sent as a message)

## How We Use Your Data

- To save deleted and edited messages
- To save photos where technically permitted by Telegram’s platform and Business Connection features
- To notify you about deletions and edits
- To provide the core functionality of the bot

## Data Storage and Encryption

- All messages and files are **encrypted** using AES encryption before storage.
- Data is stored on secure servers.
- We use temporary storage:
  - **Messages**: stored for **7 days** (1 week)
  - **Chats**: stored for **14 days** (2 weeks)
- Retention may be extended up to retention period if needed for active recovery, after which data is permanently deleted.

## Data Deletion

- Data is **automatically deleted** after the retention period.
- You can request manual deletion of all your data at any time by contacting the developer.
- After deletion, data cannot be recovered.

## Data Sharing

We **do not** sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except:
- When required by law.
- To protect our rights and safety.

## Security

- All sensitive data is encrypted at rest.
- We use secure connections (HTTPS).
- Access to stored data is strictly limited to the bot developer for maintenance only.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Request access to your stored data.
- Request deletion of your data.
- Withdraw consent by disconnecting the bot from Business Connection (your data will be deleted after the retention period).
